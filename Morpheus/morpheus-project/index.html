How it works

1. You paste text

The text is analyzed on the server running the tool.

2. Two models score it

A TF-IDF baseline and a fine-tuned DistilBERT classifier, trained on public datasets.

3. You get an estimate

HUMAN AI

AI and human likelihoods, with a warning when the result is uncertain or the text is short.

FAQ

Can it prove someone used AI?

No. It gives a statistical estimate that can be wrong. Do not use it as the sole basis for any decision about a person.

When is it most likely to be wrong?

Short text, edited or paraphrased AI text, translated text, formal writing, writing by non-native speakers, and text from newer AI models than it was trained on.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no fees, subscriptions or paid features.

Do you store my text?

No. See the Privacy section.
