03 / QUESTIONS

NO MAGIC.
JUST SIGNAL.

Can it prove someone used AI?

No. It gives a statistical estimate that can be wrong. Do not use it as the sole basis for any decision about a person.

When is it most likely to be wrong?

Short text, edited or paraphrased AI text, translated text, formal writing, writing by non-native speakers, and text from newer AI models than it was trained on.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no fees, subscriptions, or paid features in this project.

Do you store my text?

No. See the Privacy section below and review your server logs and deployment configuration before launch.
